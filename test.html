⚠️ Before You Update the Board

Read these 3 rules carefully before making your first update:

  • Updates the Live Center Board: Your click updates the live rotation for everyone in the center—not just your own phone.
  • ONLY Click Drivers Who Got Off: Do not click the people in between who stayed or worked. The board automatically skips them and highlights the next person up.
  • Your Name Is Logged: Every click stamps your name on the board. False or accidental entries will get your edit access locked.
